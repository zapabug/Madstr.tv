# Madstr.tv style card — "Jetsons meets funky stickers"

Use this as the context block for any designer or AI: it is the vibe you loved,
measured from your own files (JUKESTR 3000 mockup + the winning v2 mark).

## The vibe, named
- **Atompunk / googie** — Jetsons space-age shapes: boomerangs, starbursts, orbit rings
- **Y2K skeuomorphic gloss** — molded plastic, bevel edges, specular top-left highlights
- **Die-cut sticker / graffiti toon** — thick outlines, hard zero-blur offset shadows
- **Cassette-futurism / jukebox chunk** — chunky rounded panels, kitchy toy energy

## Mark geometry (measured from the winning v2 mark)
- Magenta `#DC01C0` play triangle, rotated ~30° clockwise, pointing right
- Lemon-cream `#FCFA9A` lightning bolt: sharp point upper-right, mid notch,
  long tapering tail to lower-left
- Outline `#141414`, ~1.7% of canvas
- Hard shadow, zero blur, down-right: dx 2.4% / dy 3.0% of canvas
- Shadow colour = surface darkened to ~45%: `#630056` on the triangle,
  `#050505` on dark backgrounds, `#610054` on white

## Palette (from the JUKESTR 3000 mockup)
- Deep purple `#4A2A8A` backdrop (radial glow), orange `#E8823A` panels/trim,
  dark slate `#2B2440` screens, mint `#66D9A6` tags, neon pink `#F04FA0` accents,
  cream `#F5E9D8` text strips, magenta `#DC01C0`, lemon `#FCFA9A`

## One-liner to paste into any brief
Chunky die-cut-sticker mark: magenta play triangle rotated 30° with a cream
lightning bolt, thick `#141414` outlines, hard zero-blur shadows down-right,
glossy Y2K molded-plastic bevel highlights, googie starburst/orbit accents,
deep purple backdrop — Jetsons meets funky sticker.
